I am an evolutionary geneticist and a postdoctoral fellow in the Department of Ecology and Evolutionary Biology at UCLA, where I work with Karen Sears. I develop theory and computational methods, grounded in genomic and phenotypic data, to understand how genotype–phenotype maps structure genetic variation and what the resulting genetic architecture reveals about natural selection.
